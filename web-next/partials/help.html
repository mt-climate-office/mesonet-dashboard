The Montana Mesonet Dashboard

A service of the Montana Climate Office.

Welcome

This dashboard shows current and historical data from every station in the Montana Mesonet. To see a station, pick it with the station button at the top, or add it to the address (for example ?s=crowagen).

Using the dashboard

  • Pick a station with the station button at the top (search by name, county, reservation, town or ZIP, Near me, recent stations or the map). The dashboard reopens your last station next time.
  • Now shows the station's current conditions, the last 48 hours with the NWS forecast, and the latest photos. Tap a reading to chart it.
  • Charts lists every variable the station reports and the Ag tools (growing degree days, feels like, livestock risk, soil profile, soil water potential and more). Compare stacks several variables on one time axis.
  • A chart's ⋯ menu shows it as a table, downloads its data as CSV, or shares it.
  • About gives the station's details, its location, every current reading and its sensor changes.
  • The address keeps the dashboard's state, so copying it (or using Share this view) shares what you see.
  • Satellite indicators are on the previous dashboard.

Data source

Station data come from the Montana Mesonet API at quality-control level 2; the newest readings are provisional until the daily quality check. Times are Mountain Time.

Montana Mesonet background

The Montana Climate Office (MCO) installed the Mesonet's first weather and soil moisture monitoring stations in 2016 as part of the Montana Research and Economic Development Initiative (MREDI). The Mesonet was designed to support decision-making for statewide drought assessments, precision agriculture, and rangeland and forested watershed management. The network grew through support from private landowners, watershed groups, tribes, state agencies and federal grants, and through a 2020 U.S. Army Corps of Engineers contract that added stations across central and eastern Montana to improve drought assessments and flood forecasting.
