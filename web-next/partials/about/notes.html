About the data

Quality control
Every value in this dashboard is quality-controlled (QC level 2): the daily QC pipeline removes spikes, steps, stuck sensors and wind-affected precipitation. Download also offers the raw and provisional levels.
Provisional data
The newest observations, usually the last day, have not been through the daily pipeline yet. They are marked Provisional on Now and in the provisional column of downloads, and they may still change.
Times and units
Times are Mountain Time. Values are in US units as the Mesonet API sends them; “Feels like” is the NWS wind chill or heat index when one applies, otherwise the air temperature.
