About the data

Quality control
The dashboard requests quality-controlled data (QC level 2): the daily QC pipeline removes spikes, steps, stuck sensors and wind-affected precipitation. Download also offers the raw and provisional levels.
Provisional data
Observations since the last daily QC run (about 8 AM) have had only basic range checks, so they are served at QC level 1. They are flagged by the ⓘ on Now and in the provisional column of downloads, and are replaced by level 2 values after the next run.
Times and units
Times are Mountain Time. Values are in US units as the Mesonet API sends them; “Feels like” is the NWS wind chill or heat index when one applies, otherwise the air temperature.
