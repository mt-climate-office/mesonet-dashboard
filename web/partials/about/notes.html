About the data

Quality control
The dashboard shows quality-controlled data, QC level 2. A daily check removes spikes, steps, stuck sensors and wind-affected precipitation. Download also offers raw and provisional data.
Provisional data
Readings since the last daily check, about 8 AM, have basic range checks only. They are QC level 1. Now marks them “Provisional”. Downloads flag them in the provisional column. Level 2 values replace them after the next check.
Times and units
Times are Mountain Time. Units in the ⋯ menu switches between US and metric. “Feels like” is the NWS wind chill or heat index when one applies. Otherwise it is the air temperature.
