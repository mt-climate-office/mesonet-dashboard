The Montana Mesonet Dashboard

What this shows

Weather and soil data from one Montana Mesonet station.

  • Now: current conditions, the last 48 hours and the forecast.
  • Charts: every variable, the Ag tools and Compare.
  • About: the station's details and sensor changes.

How to use it

  • Tap the station name to search, or use Near me or Browse on the map.
  • Tap a reading on Now to chart it.
  • Use a chart's ⋮ menu to Download data, Show as table or Share this chart.
  • Keys: Esc closes a menu, sheet or this window.

Share or bookmark

The address holds this view. Copy it, or use Share this view in the ⋯ menu. Add a station ID to open it, like ?s=crowagen. Times are Mountain Time.

Data

  • Montana Mesonet API, checked every 5 minutes.
  • National Weather Service forecasts.

The newest readings are provisional until the daily quality check.
